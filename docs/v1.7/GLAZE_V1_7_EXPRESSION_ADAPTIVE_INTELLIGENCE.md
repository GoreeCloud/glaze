# Glaze V1.7 — Expression and Adaptive Intelligence Expansion

## Direction

Glaze V1.7 evolves GoreeCloud's shared design and experience system into a more expressive, adaptive, personalized, context-aware, and continuity-focused design system.

The interface may change **shape, scale, typography, containment, material, color, composition, motion, density, and input mapping** as context changes, while preserving the user's task, intent, accessibility requirements, preferences, and authoritative system state.

This supplement remains the requirements authority for Section 48. Bounded source implementation now exists for every planned Section 48 surface, but this document does not itself establish qualification, release, deployment, consumer eligibility, or production acceptance.

## Glaze Expression System

The Glaze Expression System coordinates geometry, scale, typography, containment, material, color, composition, and motion as one governed presentation model.

It includes:

- **Semantic Geometry** — governed shape roles communicate purpose, hierarchy, relationship, and state.
- **Visual Scale and Emphasis** — Supporting, Standard, Prominent, and Hero roles provide visual hierarchy without confusing prominence with semantic severity.
- **Expressive Typography** — responsive, hierarchy-aware typography may become more distinctive while preserving readability, localization, and accessibility.
- **Semantic Containment** — spacing, surfaces, boundaries, material, elevation, geometry, and composition consistently group and separate content.
- **Component Expression** — reusable components adapt their visual character according to role, importance, form factor, theme, accessibility, input environment, and context.
- **Expression Resolution** — a governed resolver coordinates expression so applications request semantic presentation intent instead of arbitrarily manipulating raw design values.

Expression must strengthen hierarchy, comprehension, continuity, recognition, and identity before increasing visual richness.

## Adaptive Experience Layer

### Glaze Contextual Actions

Glaze Contextual Actions surface provider-supplied, context-relevant actions without manufacturing user intent, authorization, permission, consent, action availability, or execution success.

Presentation never grants authority to perform an action.

### Glaze Brief

Glaze Brief provides personalized, rearrangeable semantic cards for provider-owned information such as activities, events, tasks, media, device information, synchronization, security, privacy, recovery, and other approved state.

Personalization may change ordering, density, emphasis, composition, and expression. It must not change the underlying truth or suppress required critical communication.

### Glaze Control Center

Glaze Control Center provides configurable system-control surfaces composed from reusable semantic controls.

Control availability, current state, permissions, authorization, policy, execution, and result truth remain owned by the responsible system or provider.

### Glaze Workspace

Glaze Workspace makes one-, two-, three-, and multi-pane composition a first-class governed capability.

Workspace recomposition must preserve applicable navigation, focus, selection, scroll position, drafts, filters, pane identity, media context, and safe pending interactions. Pane count is a presentation decision, not a reason to reset application state.

### Glaze Compact Surface

Glaze Compact Surface provides glanceable and interactive presentation for cover displays, wearables, widgets, secondary displays, TV overlays, and other constrained surfaces.

Compact presentation must preserve essential meaning, accessible interaction, authoritative state, and clear escalation into a fuller experience when required.

### Glaze Agent Activity

Glaze Agent Activity provides transparent presentation of AI and agent access, actions, providers, scopes, results, and history.

Glaze presents authoritative agent/provider records; it does not infer that access occurred, an action succeeded, a scope was granted, or a result is trustworthy without responsible-provider evidence.

### Glaze Privacy Attention

Glaze Privacy Attention provides prominent but truthful privacy and security presentation driven only by authoritative provider state.

Privacy Shield remains authoritative for applicable privacy truth. Wardveil Security remains authoritative for applicable security and protection truth. Prominence must not exaggerate ordinary conditions merely to attract attention.

### Glaze Accessibility Presentation

Glaze Accessibility Presentation provides reusable capabilities such as text spotlighting, enlarged presentation, pointer assistance, keyboard continuity, voice access, switch access, Reduced Motion, Increased Contrast, Reduced Transparency, and Forced Colors.

Accessibility always outranks optional expression, motion richness, translucency, density, and decorative presentation.

### Glaze Creative Surface

Glaze Creative Surface provides reusable presentation patterns for generative and assisted creation, transformation, editing, preview, comparison, and approval.

The surface must distinguish proposed content, previews, edits, pending changes, and committed results. Generation is not approval, preview is not commitment, suggestion is not user intent, and model output is not authoritative application state.

### Glaze Compare

Glaze Compare provides side-by-side and before/after comparison patterns for themes, generated content, edits, settings, layouts, and other reversible changes.

Comparison must preserve source/target identity, remain understandable without color or motion alone, and never imply selection or commitment unless the responsible system reports that state.

### Glaze Care Surface

Glaze Care Surface provides common presentation patterns for device, application, storage, performance, energy, maintenance, recovery, and service information.

It may organize and explain supplied state, but it must not manufacture device health, maintenance status, recovery state, storage safety, energy state, performance state, or service truth.

## Signature Motion Integration

The existing Signature Motion vocabulary remains part of V1.7:

- Glaze Bloom
- Glaze Flow
- Glaze Lift
- Glaze Veil
- Glaze Fold
- Glaze Trace
- Glaze Settle
- Glaze Focus Transfer
- Glaze Color Shift
- Glaze Material Shift

Motion expression continues to support Calm, Balanced, Expressive, and Reduced Motion behavior while coordinating with the broader expression system.

Motion remains semantic and relationship-driven. It must not become a decorative substitute for state or comprehension.

## Adaptive Intelligence Authority Boundary

Adaptive intelligence is presentation-focused.

Glaze may present context, suggestions, capabilities, AI activity, and provider-supplied state, but it must never manufacture:

- user intent;
- consent;
- authorization;
- permission;
- privacy status;
- security status;
- identity;
- connectivity;
- availability;
- success;
- completion;
- recovery;
- other provider-owned truth.

When authority is missing, ambiguous, stale, conflicting, or unsupported, Glaze must fail closed or present an explicitly unknown/unavailable state rather than infer a positive condition.

## Accessibility, Performance, and Energy Precedence

Accessibility always outranks optional expression.

Performance, thermal, power, and energy constraints may reduce optional visual richness, material effects, motion, background presentation, and other non-essential effects.

Those constraints must not reduce task continuity, direct manipulation, semantic meaning, accessibility, authoritative state, essential interaction, or required privacy/security communication.

## Personalization and Truth

Personalization may change expression.

**Personalization must not change truth.**

Themes, expression profiles, layouts, ordering, density, color atmosphere, materials, typography, geometry, and motion preferences remain subordinate to accessibility and protected semantic/provider state.

## Cross-Platform Identity

Glaze V1.7 should feel recognizably GoreeCloud across Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, Foldable, TV, Wearable, compact displays, Web, native applications, and future supported form factors without requiring visual uniformity between platforms.

Common semantic identity should survive native adaptation.

## Goal

The goal is not "more glass," "more animation," or "more AI."

The goal is a system that feels **more alive, more personal, more capable, more understandable, and more continuous** while remaining controlled, accessible, performant, private, and truthful.
