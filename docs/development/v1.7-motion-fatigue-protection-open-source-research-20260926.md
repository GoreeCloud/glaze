## GoreeCloud-native evidence examined

### V1.6 focus and motion foundation

The existing GoreeCloud V1.6 focus/motion source defines the reference motion-budget dimensions already used by Glaze:

- simultaneous transitions: 6
- skeleton motion elements: 4
- background material animations: 1
- decorative movements: 2
- large-area transformations: 1
- continuous animated elements: 4

Section 32 preserves these limits rather than silently increasing them. The V1.6 resolver already reduces optional motion when the budget is exceeded and preserves direct manipulation under Reduced Motion.

### V1.6 loading/skeleton foundation

The loading resolver already turns skeleton motion static when Reduced Motion applies, when many skeletons are active, or when the animation budget is unavailable. Section 32 generalizes that fatigue behavior across the complete V1.7 motion language without changing loading-state truth.

### V1.7 Signature Microinteractions

Dev.19 already suppresses travel, scale, and icon motion under repeated-action fatigue and preserves authoritative action/result state. Section 32 keeps that behavior as an explicit input to global fatigue reduction.

## GoreeCloud requirements derived from the research

1. Preserve the established V1.6 reference motion-budget dimensions and limits; V1.7 Section 32 must not silently expand them.
2. Count simultaneous transitions, skeleton motion, background material animation, decorative movement, large-area transformations, and continuous animated elements independently.
3. When a dimension exceeds its budget, reduce optional presentation automatically without reducing semantic state.
4. Reduce decorative and continuous motion before simplifying task-relevant transitions.
5. Excess skeleton motion becomes static skeleton presentation; excess background material animation becomes static material presentation.
6. Excess large-area transformations degrade to simpler state transitions; excess simultaneous transitions simplify or immediately resolve.
7. Major task transitions suspend optional decorative movement.
8. Repeated-action pressure suppresses optional repeated microinteraction motion.
9. Reduced Motion outranks the ordinary budget and preserves direct manipulation tracking.
10. Applications may report current semantic motion counts but may not raise or override the governed budget.
11. Final state, focus, navigation, task continuity, provider truth, and input responsiveness must not depend on optional animation.

## Independent GoreeCloud design

Dev.24 creates a presentation-only budget resolver around the inherited V1.6 limits. It returns the observed budget pressure, exceeded dimensions, and deterministic reduction actions. The resolver does not execute animation, mutate application state, invent performance measurements, or grant applications a budget-control API.

The reduction order intentionally removes the least task-essential motion first: decorative movement, continuous animation, skeleton motion, background material animation, large-area transformation richness, then excess simultaneous transition richness. Direct manipulation remains input-driven even under Reduced Motion or budget pressure.

This research record documents engineering influence only. No upstream source code, exact third-party motion values, curves, components, assets, or distinctive visual identity is reproduced.
