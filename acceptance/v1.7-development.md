## Purpose

This record defines the bounded Development qualification-control model for V1.7. It does not establish V1.7 acceptance, lifecycle promotion, consumer eligibility, deployment acceptance, or production acceptance.

Section 46 contains 37 qualification dimensions. Thirty-five are required. **Wearable where claimed** and **Energy behavior where applicable** are conditional and may be marked not applicable only with an explicit, specific justification. Required lanes cannot be disabled.

## Exact-revision evidence rule

Every verified evidence record must be bound to the same exact 40-character reviewed source revision and include a non-empty evidence reference. Missing evidence remains unverified. Revision mismatches remain unverified. Partial evidence groups remain unverified.

The evidence vocabulary is bounded to machine, rendered, device, human, assistive-technology, performance, energy, and provenance evidence. Individual lanes require one or more evidence groups. When a lane has multiple groups, every group must be satisfied on the same exact reviewed revision.

For motion-related qualification, automated tests alone are insufficient. Signature Motion, connected transformations, interruption, reversal, and Reduced Motion require a separate rendered or human evidence group in addition to machine evidence.

## Acceptance-control workflow integrity

The Section 46 acceptance-control workflow is itself bounded as evidence infrastructure. It runs on the explicit `ubuntu-24.04` runner, checks out the exact pull-request or push revision with persisted checkout credentials disabled, verifies the checked-out commit matches the expected revision, and uses the repository-approved immutable `actions/setup-node` pin with Node.js 22. Validation jobs are time-bounded and fail if validation mutates tracked source.

These CI safeguards improve reproducibility and source-to-check binding only. They do not manufacture qualification evidence, satisfy any V1.7 lane, establish Section 46 completion, or change the V1.6 Anchor / V1.7 Development lifecycle boundary.

## Qualification evidence packet intake

The repository provides `acceptance/v1.7-qualification-evidence.template.json` plus `scripts/validate_glaze_v1_7_qualification_evidence.mjs` as a fail-closed intake path for real Section 46 evidence. The packet binds every verified observation to one frozen exact source revision, restricts evidence types to the lane's declared evidence groups, requires durable references, findings, observation time, reviewer identity, and a governed packet review decision, and preserves the same conditional-lane rules as the matrix.

The template contains no verified evidence. The validator's complete-matrix fixture is a self-test only and is never written as acceptance evidence. The validator also emits a deterministic `blockingLanes` gap report for every unresolved lane, including its failure reason, complete required evidence groups, and the groups still missing from the supplied packet. This report is an evidence-collection aid only; it does not verify reviewer trust, manufacture observations, waive required groups, or change applicability.

The qualification packet runtime validator now also enforces the JSON Schema's closed structural shape: exact packet/review/evidence/authority fields are required, unsupported fields fail closed, and every supplied evidence item—including an unverified item—must carry a schema-valid content-addressed reference, timezone-qualified observation time, bounded finding, and bounded reviewer identity. This prevents malformed unverified records from bypassing the retained packet structure.

Verified packet evidence references must use content-addressed `evidence+sha256:<digest>:<logical-locator>` form with a credential-safe repository-style relative logical locator. Transport URLs, URI-style schemes, Windows drive-letter paths, query strings, fragments, user-info, encoded locator material, assignment-style parameters, absolute paths, empty/traversal path segments, and equivalent credential-bearing retrieval syntax are rejected. Verified observation timestamps must be timezone-qualified and may not postdate the packet's accepted review timestamp. These controls protect retained provenance and review ordering; they do not establish that an external reviewer is authorized or that an observation is substantively correct.

Even a real packet with all 35 required lanes externally verified and both conditional lanes either verified or specifically justified can become only **ready for governed qualification review**. It does not make Section 46 complete, establish V1.7 acceptance, or grant Anchor, consumer, deployment, or production authority.

## Authority boundary

The resolver summarizes supplied evidence. It does not verify the trustworthiness of an external reviewer merely because a record says `verified: true`; the governing review process remains authoritative for evidence acceptance. A syntactically complete matrix is only **ready for governed qualification review**.

Even a complete evidence inventory does not make Section 46 complete, does not establish V1.7 acceptance, does not change `VERSION` or `registry/lifecycle.json`, and does not authorize Release Candidate, Anchor, consumer, deployment, or production status.

Glaze V1.7 / `1.7.0` is now the bounded Official Anchor / Stable-compatibility release. This retained dev.39 acceptance model continues only as historical exact-source qualification infrastructure under V1.7.1 Development; it does not expand the V1.7.0 Stable runtime, automatically rebind retained evidence to a changed V1.7.1 candidate, or grant any successor lifecycle authority. GLAZE UI V1.6 / `1.6.0` remains the immediate known-good rollback baseline.
