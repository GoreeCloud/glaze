# GLAZE UI V1.7 Development Acceptance Control

**Status:** Development source framework  
**Version:** `1.7.0-dev.39`  
**Plan:** V1.7 v1.2 Section 46  
**Stable baseline:** GLAZE UI V1.6 / `1.6.0`  
**Consumer eligibility:** No

## Purpose

This record defines the bounded Development qualification-control model for V1.7. It does not establish V1.7 acceptance, lifecycle promotion, consumer eligibility, deployment acceptance, or production acceptance.

Section 46 contains 37 qualification dimensions. Thirty-five are required. **Wearable where claimed** and **Energy behavior where applicable** are conditional and may be marked not applicable only with an explicit, specific justification. Required lanes cannot be disabled.

## Exact-revision evidence rule

Every verified evidence record must be bound to the same exact 40-character reviewed source revision and include a non-empty evidence reference. Missing evidence remains unverified. Revision mismatches remain unverified. Partial evidence groups remain unverified.

The evidence vocabulary is bounded to machine, rendered, device, human, assistive-technology, performance, energy, and provenance evidence. Individual lanes require one or more evidence groups. When a lane has multiple groups, every group must be satisfied on the same exact reviewed revision.

For motion-related qualification, automated tests alone are insufficient. Signature Motion, connected transformations, interruption, reversal, and Reduced Motion require a separate rendered or human evidence group in addition to machine evidence.

## Authority boundary

The resolver summarizes supplied evidence. It does not verify the trustworthiness of an external reviewer merely because a record says `verified: true`; the governing review process remains authoritative for evidence acceptance. A syntactically complete matrix is only **ready for governed qualification review**.

Even a complete evidence inventory does not make Section 46 complete, does not establish V1.7 acceptance, does not change `VERSION` or `registry/lifecycle.json`, and does not authorize Release Candidate, Anchor, consumer, deployment, or production status.

V1.6 / `1.6.0` remains the Official Anchor until a separate governed V1.7 qualification and promotion process becomes authoritative.
