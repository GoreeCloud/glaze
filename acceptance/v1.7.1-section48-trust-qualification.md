# Glaze V1.7.1 — Section 48 Trust Qualification Procedure

**Lifecycle:** DevelopmentQualification  
**Evidence types:** `provider-integration`, `privacy-security`  
**Frozen source:** `4b9d085a5177b96cc31d4270b38d792a59872e37` / `1.7.0-dev.47`  
**Current Stable baseline:** `1.7.0`  
**Historical source baseline:** `1.6.0`

## Purpose

This procedure governs manual collection of the two Section 48 trust-specific evidence types that are not established by machine, rendered, device, assistive-technology, human, performance, energy, or provenance controls.

A completed record is candidate evidence only. It does not become accepted evidence until a separate governed review accepts the exact-source observation and durable provenance is recorded.

## General rules

Use a real integration build and real provider/privacy/security integration. CI, fixtures, mocks, schemas, templates, and source validators are control-plane evidence only.

Record one Section 48 lane per candidate record. Bind the record to the frozen Glaze source, the exact qualification-tooling revision, and the exact integration revision being observed.

Do not record reusable credentials, bearer tokens, passwords, private keys, recovery codes, authentication headers, secret-file contents, unnecessary private payloads, or unrelated personal information. Sanitize findings while preserving enough non-secret detail to reproduce and review them.

A `session-pass` decision requires every governed scenario to pass and every required structural safeguard to be true. It still does not close the lane automatically.

## Provider-integration evidence

Eligible lanes:

- `agent-activity-authority-v13`
- `care-authority-v13`
- `provider-integration-v13`
- `contextual-actions-v13`
- `brief-v13`
- `control-center-v13`

Use `acceptance/v1.7.1-section48-provider-integration-evidence.template.json`.

The live session must verify:

1. **Source and integration identity** — confirm frozen Glaze source, exact tooling revision, provider/consumer build identities, and the contract or endpoint under review.
2. **Provider identity and provenance** — provider identity and provider-owned truth must be externally authoritative rather than invented by Glaze.
3. **Least-privilege contract** — expose only fields and actions required for the approved purpose; prefer read-only access when writes are not required; do not use direct provider database access as the integration contract.
4. **Provider truth authority** — Glaze may present provider truth but must not manufacture provider state, ranking, completion, permission, consent, authorization, or success/failure.
5. **Execution and result boundary** — explicit user intent may form a proposal, but Glaze must not execute provider commands or assume a result unless the responsible provider/application reports it authoritatively.
6. **Failure isolation and revocation** — provider unavailability, malformed payloads, or revoked access must fail safely without broadening access, exposing internal details, or creating false truth. Revocation must remove future visibility/access.
7. **Data minimization and secret exclusion** — responses and evidence must exclude credentials and unnecessary private payloads.
8. **Contract versioning and compatibility** — the integration must identify an explicit supported contract/version or equally explicit compatibility boundary and fail safely on incompatible data.

## Privacy-security evidence

Eligible lanes:

- `privacy-attention-authority-v13`
- `privacy-security-integration-v13`

Use `acceptance/v1.7.1-section48-privacy-security-evidence.template.json`.

The live session must verify:

1. **Source and integration identity** — bind Glaze, tooling, application/surface, Privacy Shield/Wardveil integration, and exact integration revision.
2. **Privacy by default and data minimization** — the observed integration is restrictive by default and exposes only information required for the approved purpose.
3. **Wardveil security authority** — only Wardveil Security may establish system-wide security-protection truth in the governed Glaze domain.
4. **Privacy Shield authority** — Privacy Shield retains system privacy-consent/access authority; responsible providers may expose only their approved provider-local privacy truth.
5. **Provider-local scope boundary** — a responsible provider must not escalate provider-local state into system-wide privacy or security authority.
6. **Conflict and unattested fail-closed behavior** — conflicting or unattested truth must resolve to unknown/withheld presentation rather than inferred precedence.
7. **Truth/presentation separation** — presentation, motion, color, material, emphasis, or animation must not manufacture privacy/security truth.
8. **Permission revocation and denial** — revoked/denied state must be observable, remain authoritative, and disable or withhold actions that require unavailable permission.
9. **Telemetry and secret exclusion** — the qualification must not require telemetry or record reusable secrets or unnecessary private content.

## Failure handling

Use `session-fail` when any required scenario fails, is not executed, cannot be verified, or reveals a blocking authority/privacy/security defect. Document the blocking finding without including secrets.

Do not convert an incomplete session into `session-pass`. Do not infer provider, privacy, or security acceptance from green automated source validation.

## Governed review boundary

A structurally valid `session-pass` record remains candidate evidence. Separate review must determine whether the environment/integration is representative, findings are sufficient, limitations are acceptable, the exact source/revisions match the intended qualification, and the record may be durably accepted for the named Section 48 lane.

No candidate record by itself establishes Section 48 acceptance, V1.7.1 lifecycle promotion, consumer eligibility, deployment acceptance, release publication, or production acceptance.
