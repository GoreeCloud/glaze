# Glaze V1.7.1 — Section 48 Field Evidence Qualification Procedure

**Lifecycle:** DevelopmentQualification
**Evidence types:** `human`, `device`, `assistive-technology`, `performance`, `energy`
**Frozen source:** `4b9d085a5177b96cc31d4270b38d792a59872e37` / `1.7.0-dev.47`
**Current Stable baseline:** `1.7.0`
**Historical source baseline:** `1.6.0`

## Purpose

This procedure governs candidate collection for the Section 48 field-evidence groups not supplied by machine, rendered, provenance, provider-integration, or privacy-security controls.

The existing retained V1.7.1 Human, Device, Assistive Technology, Performance, and Energy controls remain the governing methodological precedents. Their evidence is **not automatically inherited** by Section 48. A Section 48 record must explicitly target a v1.3 lane and include observations of the relevant Section 48 behavior.

A completed record is candidate evidence only. It does not become accepted evidence until a separate governed review accepts the exact-source observation and durable provenance is recorded.

## Common rules

Use a real integration build that contains the frozen Glaze source under review. Bind every record to the frozen Glaze source, exact qualification-tooling revision, exact integration revision, build identity, operator, environment, limitations, and one eligible Section 48 lane.

Do not use placeholders in submitted records. Do not record credentials, tokens, passwords, private keys, recovery codes, authentication headers, secret-file contents, or unrelated private data.

A `session-pass` or `review-required` candidate decision does not close a lane. CI, templates, validators, self-tests, emulators, simulators, screenshots alone, accessibility-tree inspection alone, synthetic timing, or proxy-only energy observations are not field evidence.

## Human evidence

Use `acceptance/v1.7.1-section48-human-evidence.template.json`.

A real human observer must exercise and inspect the lane-specific Section 48 behavior. Where interaction is part of the behavior, use real interaction rather than scripted activation. Record what was observed, what was not covered, and any blocking finding.

For authority-bearing surfaces, confirm provider-owned truth remains externally authoritative and Glaze presentation does not manufacture permission, consent, authorization, completion, failure, privacy, or security truth.

For `cross-platform-expression-v13`, the human record must cover at least two distinct platform families and assess semantic consistency without requiring mechanical uniformity.

## Device evidence

Use `acceptance/v1.7.1-section48-device-evidence.template.json`.

Device evidence requires real physical hardware. Emulators, simulators, responsive browser viewport emulation, and hosted CI do not qualify.

- `workspace-continuity-v13`: observe real window/form-factor or posture transition and task continuity.
- `compact-surface-v13`: use a real constrained/compact physical device or hardware mode.
- `large-text-reflow-v13`: use a real platform text-size setting at 200% or the closest governed platform equivalent.
- `compact-device-behavior-v13`: observe representative compact-device behavior on actual hardware.
- `cross-platform-expression-v13`: cover at least two distinct platform families; one device cannot establish cross-platform consistency.
- `energy-v13`: Device evidence is only the independent Device group and does not establish Energy evidence.

## Assistive-technology evidence

Use `acceptance/v1.7.1-section48-assistive-technology-evidence.template.json`.

Use real assistive technology on a physical device and record technology name, version, input mode, platform, and scenario observations. Accessibility-tree inspection, automated keyboard traversal, and synthetic semantics are control-plane evidence only.

`switch-access-v13` requires an actual switch-access technology/path. `voice-access-v13` requires an actual voice-access technology/path. For compact/accessibility/large-text/Forced Colors lanes, verify the claimed assistive technology can reach, understand, and operate the relevant Section 48 surface without losing task or semantic truth.

## Performance evidence

Use `acceptance/v1.7.1-section48-performance-evidence.template.json`.

Measure a representative foreground environment using the approved **GoreeCloud Standard — Glaze UI Performance Budget v1.0** and **System-Wide Motion Continuity and Performance** authority. The measurement must include real Section 48 workload coverage, not only generic V1.7 resolver work.

At minimum, collect 200 resolver samples, 30 real interaction-to-painted-update samples, 120 idle frame intervals, and 240 active frame intervals. The record must identify the Section 48 surfaces exercised and must preserve task state and authority boundaries throughout the run.

Hosted CI is not representative-performance evidence. A passing companion V1.2 performance run does not automatically satisfy `performance-v13`; Section 48-specific workload coverage and separate governed review are required.

## Energy evidence

Use `acceptance/v1.7.1-section48-energy-evidence.template.json`.

Use a real physical device and direct quantitative energy or power measurement. Battery percentage, thermal state, CPU/GPU utilization, or frame rate alone are contextual/proxy signals and do not satisfy Energy evidence.

Measure controlled idle, representative foreground Section 48 workload, constrained/low-power operation, background/off-screen lifecycle, and thermal/resource recovery using the same device/build/method where practical. Record the Section 48 surfaces exercised.

No numeric Energy acceptance threshold is currently approved for this lane. Do not invent one. A structurally complete record remains `review-required` for governed assessment. The separate Device evidence group for `energy-v13` remains independently required.

## Section 48 coverage

Field evidence should cover the applicable surfaces and boundaries, including Expression System, Contextual Actions, Glaze Brief, Glaze Control Center, Workspace, Compact Surface, Accessibility Presentation, Agent Activity, Privacy Attention, Care Surface, Creative Surface, and Compare.

Also preserve provider-owned truth, privacy/security truth, user intent versus provider result, Large Text, Forced Colors, keyboard/switch/voice access where applicable, and continuity through expanded/compact, input, and accessibility changes.

## Failure handling

Use `session-fail`, `measurement-blocked`, or an equivalent fail-closed candidate decision when a required scenario cannot be executed, the environment is not representative, exact identity is unavailable, a blocking defect appears, or the method is not valid for the evidence type.

Do not convert incomplete or proxy-only observations into a passing candidate. Do not infer Section 48 acceptance from green source validation.

## Governed review boundary

A structurally valid candidate remains candidate evidence. Separate review must determine whether the environment is representative, the lane-specific observations are sufficient, limitations are acceptable, exact revisions match the intended qualification, and the record may be durably accepted for the named Section 48 lane.

No candidate record by itself establishes Section 48 acceptance, V1.7.1 lifecycle promotion, consumer eligibility, deployment acceptance, release publication, or production acceptance.
