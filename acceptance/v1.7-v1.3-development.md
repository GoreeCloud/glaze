# Glaze V1.7 v1.3 — Development Qualification Boundary

**Control version:** 1.7.0-dev.43  
**Lifecycle:** Development  
**Stable baseline:** 1.6.0 Anchor  
**Consumer eligible:** No

## Purpose

dev.43 adds a v1.3 qualification-control layer for Section 48 without rewriting the frozen v1.2/dev.39 qualification contract.

The control evaluates one exact 40-character source revision across:

- the retained 37-lane v1.2 acceptance matrix; and
- 21 additional Section 48 lanes covering the v1.3 Expression and Adaptive Intelligence source.

The combined matrix contains 58 lanes.

## New Section 48 lanes

Workspace continuity; Compact Surface behavior; Agent Activity authority; Privacy Attention authority; Accessibility Presentation; Creative state separation; Compare neutrality; Care authority; Large Text reflow; Forced Colors; keyboard continuity; switch access; voice access; representative compact-device behavior; provider integration; privacy/security integration; cross-platform expression; measured performance; applicable energy behavior; human visual/interaction review; and artifact provenance.

Only `energy-v13` is conditionally applicable. Every other new lane is required.

## Evidence rules

- Evidence must be externally verified and tied to the exact candidate revision.
- Every required evidence group for every applicable lane must be satisfied.
- Required lanes cannot be declared not applicable.
- Missing, stale, mismatched, partial, or unverified evidence does not pass.
- v1.2 evidence remains governed by the existing dev.39 contract and is not rewritten by this layer.
- Automated source tests do not substitute for required rendered, device, assistive-technology, performance, provenance, or human evidence.

## Authority boundary

A complete matrix only means the exact revision is **ready for governed qualification review**. It does not establish:

- Section 48 acceptance;
- V1.7 acceptance;
- Seal;
- Anchor;
- Stable status;
- consumer eligibility;
- deployment acceptance; or
- production acceptance.

Those states remain governed by the applicable GoreeCloud release, production-readiness, visual acceptance, security/privacy, recovery, platform-integration, and Anchor-qualification standards.

## Current state

Section 48 source scope is implemented through dev.42. The dev.43 control exists to make the remaining evidence gaps explicit and exact-revision-bound. V1.7 remains Development until the required evidence is supplied and governed promotion occurs. V1.6.0 remains the current Anchor.
