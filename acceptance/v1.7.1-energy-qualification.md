# Glaze V1.7.1 — Energy-Behavior Qualification

**Lifecycle:** V1.7.1 Development qualification control only  
**Current Stable baseline:** Glaze V1.7 / `1.7.0`  
**Frozen source under review:** `4b9d085a5177b96cc31d4270b38d792a59872e37` / `1.7.0-dev.47`  
**Retained acceptance model:** `1.7.0-dev.39`  
**Evidence type:** `energy` only  
**Authority boundary:** This procedure records direct quantitative energy/power observations. It does not itself grant Energy acceptance, Device evidence, lane closure, Section 48 acceptance, lifecycle authority, consumer eligibility, publication, deployment, or production acceptance.

## Purpose

This procedure defines how a real physical-device measurement session may create a candidate `energy` evidence record for the conditional retained `energy-behavior` lane.

The retained acceptance model requires **two independent groups** for that lane:

- `energy`
- `device`

The Physical-Device Qualification control can supply only the separate `device` group after governed review. This procedure can supply only the separate `energy` group after governed review. Neither record may claim the other group automatically.

## Governing requirements

Apply the stricter applicable requirements from:

- **GoreeCloud Standard — System-Wide Motion Continuity and Performance**, especially power efficiency, off-screen/background motion, adaptive motion complexity, representative performance evidence, and the Glaze Continuity Gate.
- **GoreeCloud — Standard — Glaze UI Performance Budget v1.0**, especially exact-source/environment identity, representative hardware, constrained/low-power coverage, state integrity, local-first minimized records, and fail-closed handling of missing measurements.
- `contracts/v1.7/performance-energy-awareness.dev.json`.
- `contracts/v1.7/acceptance.dev.json`.

No numeric Energy acceptance threshold is currently defined for this retained lane. This protocol therefore **must not invent one**. A complete measurement record is `review-required`, not automatically passing.

## What qualifies as a measurement

A candidate record must use a real physical device and a direct quantitative energy or power measurement method. Permitted method families are:

- external power meter;
- platform energy instrument;
- battery energy counter;
- battery charge counter;
- platform power profiler;
- another direct quantitative method that records a named metric and unit.

Battery percentage by itself is contextual metadata, not Energy evidence. Thermal state by itself is not Energy evidence. CPU/GPU utilization by itself is not Energy evidence. An emulator, simulator, synthetic fixture, CI runner, screenshot, source validator, or qualitative statement alone is not Energy evidence.

## Exact-source and integration identity

Every candidate record must identify:

- exact Glaze source revision `4b9d085a5177b96cc31d4270b38d792a59872e37`;
- exact qualification-tooling revision;
- GoreeCloud integration repository;
- exact integration revision;
- integration build identifier;
- application under test;
- real physical device identity;
- platform/version and form factor.

The running integration must actually include the frozen Glaze revision. A measurement of a different Glaze revision cannot be relabeled.

## Required environment context

Record:

- power source;
- battery percentage before and after as context only;
- thermal state before and after;
- controlled background-load description;
- relevant ambient condition;
- whether a low-power/power-saving mode is available;
- display refresh rate when directly available.

Do not include serial numbers, account identifiers, credentials, private content, unrelated telemetry, or unrelated user activity.

## Measurement method

Use one direct quantitative method consistently across the measured scenarios in a record.

Record the method name, version/model where applicable, and enough baseline/calibration context for an independent reviewer to understand the measurement.

Measurements must include an explicit metric name, unit, and numeric value. Examples include average power in watts, consumed energy in joules or watt-hours, charge consumption in mAh when the platform exposes it directly, or another directly observed platform energy metric with its native unit. Each measured Energy scenario must run for at least 30 seconds, and the comparative scenarios must belong to the same measurement session on the same device/build/method.

A platform-specific energy score or native unit is comparable only within the same tool/session unless the platform documentation establishes a stronger comparability guarantee. Record that limitation explicitly. Do not convert a qualitative operating-system label into a fake numeric quantity.

## Required scenario sequence

### 1. Source and build identity

Confirm the running integration/build and the record identify the exact integration revision and frozen Glaze revision.

This scenario may use identity/count measurements; it is not itself the quantitative Energy observation.

### 2. Controlled idle baseline

Measure the reviewed surface in a controlled foreground idle state for a documented duration. Record the direct quantitative energy/power metric and environmental context.

The baseline exists for comparison; it is not a pass threshold.

### 3. Foreground representative workload

Exercise representative Glaze interactions and motion while measuring the same direct energy/power metric.

Include ordinary presentation and representative state changes. Do not manufacture provider, permission, authorization, capability, privacy, or security truth merely to create workload.

### 4. Constrained or low-power workload

Repeat representative interaction under an actually available constrained or low-power condition. Examples include a platform power-saving mode or a real resource-constrained presentation mode.

Observe whether optional visual richness is simplified without losing task continuity, accessibility, semantic/provider truth, direct manipulation, or final-state correctness. Exercise the applicable Reduced Motion/accessibility preference path during the session and confirm that energy adaptation does not weaken accessibility behavior.

If the target platform has no applicable constrained/low-power mode, the record is measurement-blocked for this protocol rather than silently passing.

### 5. Background/off-screen lifecycle

Place the reviewed surface into the platform's real background, obscured, minimized, off-screen, or equivalent lifecycle state where supported and measure energy/power behavior.

Observe whether optional non-visible visual work suspends or materially reduces where the platform permits it. Continuous invisible rendering without a defensible purpose is a blocking finding.

### 6. Thermal and resource recovery

After representative workload, continue measurement through recovery. Record thermal state and direct quantitative energy/power behavior during recovery.

Observe whether optional work settles rather than remaining unnecessarily active.

### 7. Authority and task integrity

Verify that performance/energy adaptation did not:

- reset task state;
- alter authoritative provider truth;
- request permission automatically;
- infer consent;
- perform consequential execution;
- change security/privacy truth;
- navigate automatically;
- create fallback execution not authorized by the application/provider.

Record an automatic-authority-action count. Any nonzero observed unauthorized automatic action is a blocking finding.

## Measurement limitations

Every record must state the material limitations of the method and session, including precision/resolution limits, platform-specific energy-unit comparability, uncontrolled environmental factors, unsupported scenarios, or other constraints that affect interpretation. Missing limitations are not treated as evidence of perfect measurement.

## Decision semantics

The protocol supports only:

- `review-required` — the required real measurement was completed structurally and must undergo separate governed evidence review.
- `measurement-blocked` — a required measurement/scenario could not be completed or the record still contains a blocking gap.

There is no `pass` value because no numeric Energy acceptance threshold has been approved for this lane.

A reviewer may later accept or reject the candidate Energy evidence based on the measured behavior and governing standards. That decision belongs in a separate durable governed evidence record, not by editing this protocol or template into an acceptance assertion.


The self-test is synthetic and is not Energy evidence.
