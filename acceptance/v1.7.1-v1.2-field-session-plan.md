4. **Physical devices + field human:** Device evidence for Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, Foldable, TV, Wearable where applicable, Touch, Native behavior, and Energy behavior; separate Human evidence for Touch and Native behavior.
5. **Direct energy measurement:** Energy evidence for Energy behavior where applicable using direct quantitative energy/power measurement.

## Boundary

Wearable and Energy behavior are conditional only with specific governed justification. CI, emulators, automation, templates, or co-scheduling cannot substitute for required Human, Device, AT, Performance, or Energy observations. Candidates must bind the frozen source and exact tooling/integration provenance, then be independently accepted before entry through the governed v1.2 evidence intake.

Control coverage, field sessions, green CI, or even a complete evidence inventory do not by themselves complete Section 46, establish V1.7.1 acceptance, promote lifecycle state, authorize publication, or establish deployment/production acceptance.
