# Contributing to Glaze

Glaze V1.7 (`1.7.0`) is GoreeCloud's current Official Stable/Anchor, consumer-eligible shared visual and interaction design system. GLAZE UI V1.6 / `1.6.0` is the immediate known-good Stable rollback baseline; earlier releases remain historical provenance.

Current Stable work must preserve accessibility, semantic clarity, platform-native behavior, exact-revision evidence, consumer-governance boundaries, presentation-only authority, and GoreeCloud repository/revision-control requirements. The V1.7.0 runtime is deliberately bounded to accepted V1.6.0 behavior; retained dev.47 and Section 48 behavior belongs to V1.7.1 Development until separately qualified.

Do not change `VERSION`, `registry/lifecycle.json`, Stable entrypoints, consumer eligibility, or downstream conformance as a side effect of ordinary implementation work. Lifecycle promotion requires a separate governed decision with exact-revision evidence. Human, representative-runtime, physical-device, accessibility, privacy/security, and performance gates may be supported by automation, but automation must not impersonate required review or acceptance authority.

The accepted V1.6 publication identity remains immutable historical evidence: source `a7180679ea851389e0f3004515f9a25f420e716d`, tag `v1.6.0`, GitHub Release `392095913`, and its recorded artifact digests. Do not rewrite or silently replace those historical release identities or bytes.

New behavior not already part of current V1.7.0 Stable authority must remain in the explicitly governed V1.7.1 Development line (or a later governed line) until formal qualification and promotion. It must include applicable contracts, tests, rendered/native evidence, accessibility coverage, privacy/security review, migration/rollback considerations, and documentation.

Downstream applications remain independently responsible for repository-local V1.7 conformance and production acceptance. Glaze Stable/Anchor status does not transfer product acceptance.

Git history remains the required revision audit trail. Historical release and Candidate records may retain original language when explicitly historical. Current front-door guidance must derive lifecycle truth from `VERSION` and `registry/lifecycle.json`.

## Retained V1.6 Release Candidate provenance

`1.6.0-rc.1` is retained as superseded, non-consumer-eligible evidence. Changes that alter its historical evidence, frozen-source bindings, or release provenance must be treated as release-sensitive history changes and independently validated.
