## Product origin and enduring direction

Glaze UI was established as GoreeCloud's first-party shared visual, interaction, accessibility, material, adaptive-layout, and component system.

Its enduring authority boundary is presentation-only: Glaze may visually represent state supplied by applications, platforms, Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, Identity, Policy, Mesh, Observability, or other providers, but it must not manufacture their truth or expand their operational authority.

The project has repeatedly refined versioning, material, color, accessibility, component, form-factor, performance, evidence, and consumer-adoption contracts while preserving that boundary.

## Consumer governance

Consumer adoption is intentionally repository-local.

A downstream GoreeCloud application must not be marked conformant merely because:
- it changes a version string;
- copies assets/tokens;
- approximates Glaze styling;
- changes a dependency;
- references the Stable release;
- had acceptance for an older Glaze version.

Consumers require fresh exact-revision implementation and applicable acceptance evidence.

The current consumer registry records V1.6.0 as the required shared target while preserving application-level fail-closed acceptance.
