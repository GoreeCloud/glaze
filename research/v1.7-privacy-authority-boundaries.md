## Purpose

This record captures exact-revision open-source research used to derive the bounded Section 44 source requirements. It is research provenance, not privacy, security, provider-integration, rendered, native-platform, assistive-technology, representative-device, or human truth-communication acceptance evidence.

Section 44 deliberately reuses the existing GoreeCloud V1.5 provider-authority registry. The new layer does not create a competing provider precedence or provenance system.

## Existing GoreeCloud authority foundation reused

Section 44 composes the existing V1.5 provider registry rather than replacing it. That registry requires provenance for non-unknown capability state, rejects provenance impersonation, enforces authority ownership, fails duplicate provider truth closed by omission, and never infers provider precedence.

The V1.7 layer adds product/domain ownership restrictions on top of those mechanics:

- Wardveil Security owns security/protection truth.
- Privacy Shield owns privacy/consent truth and system-level privacy access truth.
- Responsible providers may report only provider-local privacy access, synchronization, operation-result, and connectivity truth in their own scope.
- Everkeep owns resilience/recovery truth.
- GoreeCloud Identity owns identity/authentication truth.
- Platform sources may report platform-local connectivity/availability truth.
- GoreeCloud Mesh may report coordination status only; it does not inherit security, privacy, governance, consent, permission, or authorization authority.

## GoreeCloud-native Section 44 decisions

The dev.37 layer therefore:

1. Binds each non-unknown truth claim to explicit provider provenance, authority class, owner kind, and scope.
2. Reuses the V1.5 provider snapshot to enforce provenance, authority ownership, duplicate-conflict fail-closed behavior, and no inferred precedence.
3. Resolves conflicting or untrusted claims to unknown rather than choosing a winner.
4. Prevents protection, privacy revocation, synchronization completion, success, recovery completion, or authentication-success cues unless the corresponding accepted authoritative state exists.
5. Requires separate authoritative transition-occurrence evidence before non-trivial motion is allowed; state itself never waits for animation completion.
6. Routes accepted truth through the Section 43 visual/motion direction layer so accessibility/performance constraints can reduce presentation richness without modifying truth.
7. Rejects inferred state, confidence/probability, provider ranking/precedence, forced success, raw animation controls, pixel/screenshot scoring, and winner selection.
8. Keeps Glaze presentation-only and does not grant permission, consent, authorization, acceptance, or operational authority.
