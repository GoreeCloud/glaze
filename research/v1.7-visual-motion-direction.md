## Purpose

This record captures exact-revision open-source research used to derive the bounded Section 43 source requirements. It is research provenance, not rendered, native-platform, assistive-technology, representative-device, measured-performance, energy, or human visual-and-motion acceptance evidence.

The GoreeCloud implementation remains independent. It does not import upstream motion constants, animation values, visual assets, component implementations, effects, or design identity.

## Cross-source findings

The reviewed projects support these implementation-neutral requirements:

1. Motion should be a coherent system rather than a collection of unrelated per-component effects.
2. Richer motion should be associated with meaningful or prominent relationships, not applied universally.
3. Spatial motion, effect-like presentation changes, and material/appearance behavior should remain semantically distinguishable.
4. Platform/user accessibility and animation settings can legitimately suppress or simplify optional motion.
5. Shared motion vocabulary does not require copying another system's numeric durations, curves, springs, keyframes, or visual identity.
6. A design direction should preserve native adaptation and platform-owned settings rather than force pixel-identical presentation.
7. Optional visual richness should fail toward clarity when accessibility, certainty, or constrained performance requires it.

## GoreeCloud-native Section 43 decisions

The dev.36 layer therefore:

- Defines eight advancement objectives: adaptive composition, theme architecture, semantic color, connected transitions, spatial continuity, microinteractions, native platform adaptation, and quiet recognizable motion.
- Makes comprehension-before-spectacle, semantic-purpose-before-effect, accessibility precedence, performance precedence, truth precedence, and native adaptation explicit source policy.
- Prohibits “glass everywhere” and “animation everywhere” as default design strategies.
- Keeps material richness localized and semantic; readability, critical certainty, Reduced Transparency, Increased Contrast, and Forced Colors may require solid or semantic-equivalent presentation.
- Requires an authoritative transition occurrence before non-trivial semantic motion is selected.
- Suppresses optional decorative motion and keeps continuous decorative animation non-default.
- Allows Calm, accessibility, and authoritative performance pressure to reduce optional richness without rewriting durable preferences or authoritative application state.
- Rejects caller attempts to supply raw blur/opacity/glass coverage, duration/delay/easing/spring/keyframe/path, frame-rate/pixel/screenshot, ranking/rating/score, or winner controls.
- Preserves V1.6 / 1.6.0 Anchor authority, V1.7 non-consumer-eligibility, and the Section 42 cross-device non-uniformity boundary.

## Incorporation statement

No third-party source code, animation constants, numeric motion values, duration values, timing functions, easing curves, spring constants, keyframes, visual effects, material recipes, components, assets, fonts, icons, visual identity, trademarks, or branding are incorporated by this tranche. The sources above were used only to extract system-level requirements.

## Acceptance boundary

Source behavior and exact-source validation do not establish Section 43 completion. Section 43 remains incomplete until applicable rendered, native-platform, assistive-technology, representative-device, measured-performance, energy, cross-device, and human visual-and-motion review evidence is independently accepted.
